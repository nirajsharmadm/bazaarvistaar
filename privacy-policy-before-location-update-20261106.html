Privacy Policy

This page explains how BazaarVistaar handles information you choose to share when you contact us or enquire about our services.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

We respect your privacy. Please read this policy to understand what information may be collected through your interaction with BazaarVistaar, why it is used and how you can contact us about it.

1. Information you may share with us

When you use an enquiry form or contact us directly, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name and mobile or WhatsApp number.
  • Your business name and business location.
  • Your website, Google Business Profile or social media links.
  • The services you are interested in and the details you include in your message.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information through a general enquiry form or chat.

2. How enquiry information is used

We use the details you provide to respond to your enquiry, understand your business requirements, discuss requested services, prepare or share relevant proposals, and follow up on the conversation you initiated. We do not describe an enquiry as consent to receive unrelated promotional messages. If we send optional marketing updates or offers, you can ask us to stop.

3. Website forms and WhatsApp

When you submit an enquiry form on this website, the form prepares a message using the details you entered and opens WhatsApp. You can review the message before choosing to send it. Your details are not sent to BazaarVistaar through that form unless you choose to send the WhatsApp message.

Once you send a message, WhatsApp/Meta processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to the settings and policies of that service.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal contact information. We may share relevant information when needed to respond to your request, work with a service provider you have asked us to use, comply with a legal obligation, or protect our rights and the safety of others. We aim to share only what is reasonably necessary for that purpose.

5. Retention and security

We may retain enquiry conversations and contact details for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, provide requested services, maintain relevant business records or meet legal requirements. We take reasonable care when handling this information, but no method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and uses WhatsApp when you choose to send an enquiry. Those third-party services may process technical information under their own privacy policies. This policy does not make a claim that BazaarVistaar uses analytics or advertising cookies. If additional analytics, advertising or embedded services are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them.

7. Your choices and requests

You can contact us to ask what enquiry information you have shared with us, request correction of inaccurate details, ask us to delete information that we no longer need to retain, or ask us to stop sending optional promotional messages. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate business recordkeeping.

8. Links to other websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review the privacy information published by those services before sharing information with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or information-handling practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
