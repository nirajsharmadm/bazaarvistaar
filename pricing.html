GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing FAQs

Quick answers to common questions before you choose a service.

Are the packages one-time or monthly?

Each package is labelled as one-time, monthly or per video. One-time onboarding and setup fees are charged once; monthly management plans are recurring service fees.

Is GST included in the displayed prices?

Displayed prices are before GST. GST will be charged additionally if applicable.

Is the Google or Meta advertising budget included?

No. Advertising spend is separate and is paid directly by you to the advertising platform. Our campaign management fee covers the service work described in the selected package.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes. You can combine services based on your business goals. Contact us to discuss a suitable scope and quotation.

Can I request a customised package?

Yes. Share your requirements and budget. We’ll review the scope and confirm what can be delivered and at what price before work begins.

Are domain and hosting included in website development?

Domain registration, hosting, premium plugins, paid themes and other third-party subscriptions are not included unless specifically stated in your written quotation.

What if I need extra revisions or deliverables?

Work beyond the listed scope, including extra revisions, additional locations, languages or deliverables, will be discussed and quoted separately before proceeding.
